How to play & accessibility

Player 1
A / D move · W jump
F light · G heavy · H Spark
S hold guard

Player 2 (local)
← / → move · ↑ jump
J light · K heavy · L Spark
↓ hold guard

The rhythm
Light interrupts a slow wind-up. Heavy reaches farther. Sparks recharge in 3.5 seconds. Guard reduces incoming energy loss but chips guard; an empty guard briefly stuns. Mix jumps, quick lights and well-timed heavies.

Comfort
P / Esc pause · M mute
Touch controls support solo play. Local mode needs a keyboard; some keyboards limit simultaneous keys. System reduced-motion removes shake, particles and celebration dance. No flashing effects.

Campaign: ten stages, three match attempts per stage. A lost match spends one attempt; you may swap to another team beast. A stage win awards one heart piece and restores three attempts. Ten pieces form a full heart. Progress saves between matches, not mid-fight. Closing a match forfeits an attempt to prevent free retries. Local high scores are not online rankings.
